About Members of the Senedd

We are the Welsh Parliament

Register of Members' Interests

chevron_right

Section 36 of the Government of Wales Act 2006 and Standing Order 2 require the Senedd to maintain and publish a Register of Members’ Interests.

Register of Cross Party Groups

chevron_right

Cross-Party Groups may be set up by Members of the Senedd in respect of any subject area relevant to the Senedd.

Code of Conduct

chevron_right

The essential purpose of this Code of Conduct is to uphold the reputation of the Senedd and to provide the openness and accountability necessary to reinforce public confidence in the integrity of Members of the Senedd in the way in which they discharge their important public duties and responsibilities.
